About Namboole High School

History

A school community since 2000

Namboole High School was established in 2000 and is registered with Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports. The school is located in Bweyogerere and serves day and boarding students.

Contact the school

Learning in Bweyogerere

Namboole High School is a private Christian-founded, mixed day and boarding secondary school. Its motto, “Aspire to Inspire,” reflects a commitment to learning, character, faith, and community.

Students can study Arts and Sciences at O-Level and A-Level. The school library supports reading, research, and independent study, while the computer lab helps students build practical ICT skills. Science laboratories also support practical learning.

Why Choose Namboole High School

A school community for academic learning, personal growth, faith, and co-curricular participation.

Arts and Sciences

Students can pursue Arts and Sciences at both O-Level and A-Level.

Library and laboratories

The stocked library provides resources for reading, research, and independent study. In the computer lab, students can develop practical ICT skills. Science laboratories support hands-on learning.

Day and boarding

Namboole welcomes students as a mixed day and boarding secondary school.

Christian foundation

Faith and fellowship are part of the school's Christian-founded identity.

Student activities

Students can take part in activities such as football, choir, leadership, and fellowship.

Located in Bweyogerere

The school is in Bweyogerere, Kira Municipality, Wakiso District, Uganda.
