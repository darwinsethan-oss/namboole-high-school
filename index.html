About Namboole

A school community since 2000

Namboole High School is a Christian-founded mixed day and boarding secondary school in Bweyogerere. Students can study Arts and Sciences at O-Level and A-Level in a community committed to learning, character, and faith.

Established 2000 Day and boarding O-Level and A-Level

School leadership

Message from the Deputies

Academics & Hard Work: Deputies in charge of academics often emphasize that success comes to students who stay committed to their studies, maintain good behavior, and regularly consult their teachers.

Discipline & Character: Administration deputies frequently highlight that shaping a learner's discipline—through guidance, counseling, and spiritual or moral nurturing—is the foundation for long-term achievement.
